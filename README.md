## Research architecture

The archive separates five mathematical lanes:

1. **Angle division:** constructible approximate trisection and its local cubic
   refinement law.
2. **N-Series core:** even-power scale cancellation, general scale operators,
   and stability.
3. **Constructibility and verification:** finite constructible approximants,
   algebraic generation, and numerical consistency audits.
4. **AGM and Ramanujan structure:** dynamic coefficient laws, Landen
   contraction, and guarded modular comparisons.
5. **Ellipse geometry:** universal polygonal scaling, Ramanujan comparison,
   and residual recovery.

The repositories are deliberately separated by mathematical question. Shared
formulas do not imply duplicate papers: each repository has a distinct claim
boundary and reproducibility package.
